## How it works

This is a JK flip flop circuit that takes inputs clock, J, and K, and outputs Q and Q-not. 

## How to test

Try out different clock signals and J and K and see the output change.

## External hardware

Button for clock signal, switches for J and K, and 2 LEDs for output.
